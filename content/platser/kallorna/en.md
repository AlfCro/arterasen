People and animals needed water every day, and the milk was often cooled in cold spring water. The springs were what made the summer farm possible.

Two springs still flow today: Spjutgravskällan and Möhrkällan. The water from Spjutgravskällan is said to taste very good. Kvickkällan and Ninnis källa could be brought back to life. There are also three dug wells and one drilled well by the café.

In 2025 Möhrkällan and Bäckman's well were restored with money from a local community fund, so that the farm will have water in the future too.
