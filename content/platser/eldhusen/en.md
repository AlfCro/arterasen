The eldhus, or fire house, was home and kitchen on the summer farm. Around the open hearth people cooked their food and turned the milk into cheese, butter and messmör. Several people often shared one fire house.

In the mid-1800s there were 22 fire houses at Ärteråsen and 36 people working the farm. Twelve fire houses still stand, and they have several different owners.

Inside, the walls carry many of the farm's carvings: years, names and farm marks cut into the timber. Read more about them at sign 3.

The fire houses are still in use, for get-togethers, games and folk music during the association's culture week.
