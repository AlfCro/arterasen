The trail is a restored historic path between the village of Näset and Ärteråsen. It is 7.6 km long and signposted with a map from Brända and from the Näset village hall. Along the way there are resting stones with signs.

In the past the village moved up to the summer farm in mid-June. This was called buföring. Only the animals that were milked came along, that is cows and goats. Horses, calves, sheep and pigs stayed at home. Before then the men had been up to mend fences, chop firewood and get the houses ready.

During the summer the young men of the village came up on Saturday evenings. They carried up salt for the butter and took butter and cheese back home. The summer at the farm often lasted until the end of August. Then the animals were driven back to the village.
