When the village of Näset was divided up in a land reform, the summer farm was mapped too, with every owner's land and houses. The map was drawn in 1825 and confirmed in 1828, and the list of owners dates from 1827. At that time there were 17 farm units with 36 owners from Näset and Arvet. Brothers and sisters often owned shares together.

Compared with today, only five fire houses stand where the map shows them. Six have been moved a short distance, three stand where the map has no house, and seven of the fire houses on the map are gone. The farm units in the south-east, down by the stream, have disappeared completely.

Why move a house? The bottom log rotted from the damp ground. If the house had to be taken apart anyway to replace it, it could just as well be moved. On some houses the logs are numbered so that they would go back in the right place. Houses have also been moved here from summer farms belonging to Skattungbyn and Sunnanhed.
