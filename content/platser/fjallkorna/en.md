The Swedish mountain cow, fjällko, is an old native breed from northern Sweden and its summer farms. It is usually white with black or red patches and has no horns. It does well on forest grazing.

By grazing, the cows keep the meadow open. Without them the land would soon grow over with scrub and forest, and the farm would look completely different.

The cows roam freely on the meadow. Keep your distance and always close gates behind you. Dogs are not welcome during the weeks the cows are here.
