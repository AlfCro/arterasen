The shed stands near the café, but its timber is older than the shed. The logs probably come from a fire house that was taken down, and the shed was moved here from Skattungbyn in Orsa parish. The years carved in the timber are mostly from the 1690s and the early 1700s, older than most things on the farm.

On the inside wall opposite the door there are ten figures. Two men fight with sword and spear. A rider perhaps has an elk in front of him. Other figures show a man with a spear and two dogs, a man aiming a gun, a man with a lantern and a man with an axe beside a dog.

Most carvings on the farm are initials and years. Of more than 10,000 carvings only 66 are figures, and 25 of those are in two sheds, this one and one at another house group.
